NCF Clone & NCF Clone Pro — Privacy Policy

Packages: com.dev4u.ncf_clone_application (NCF Clone) · com.dev4u.ncf_clone_application.pro (NCF Clone Pro)
Publisher: dev4u · Last updated: 2026-11-03

NCF Clone lets you store the NFC cards you own on your own phone and present one of them to a reader. NCF Clone Pro is the same application with the advanced tools unlocked, no advertising and an optional paid upgrade. This page explains what both apps do with data.

The short version
  • Your card data never leaves your device.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours.
  • Only the free app shows ads, and only the free app uses an advertising identifier.
  • Payments in the Pro app are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

1. Card data stays on your phone

When you clone a card, the app reads its identifier, technology information and NDEF content and stores them in the app's private storage on that device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party. Deleting a card in the app removes it immediately, and uninstalling the app removes all stored cards.

Card data is never shared with the advertising SDK, with Google Play Billing, or with any other component.

2. NFC permission

The app uses the NFC radio only while you are reading a card or while you explicitly started an emulation session. On Android the app registers a Host Card Emulation service so a reader can select the clone you chose; nothing is presented unless you started that session.

3. Advertising FREE APP ONLY

NCF Clone displays a banner advertisement served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may process a device advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your IP address, and app usage signals, as described in Google's privacy policy.

NCF Clone Pro contains no advertising SDK activity: the ads SDK is never initialised and no ad request is ever made. The Pro app does not use an advertising identifier for advertising purposes.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Purchases and subscriptions PRO APP ONLY

NCF Clone Pro offers an optional monthly subscription and a one-time lifetime unlock. Both are sold and processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your name, card number, billing address or any other payment detail.

To decide whether Pro features are unlocked, the app asks Google Play for the purchase state of your Google account and stores only the resulting status on your device:

  • which product is active (monthly subscription or lifetime unlock),
  • whether a free trial is running,
  • the end of the current paid period, and
  • whether the subscription will renew.

This status is stored locally so the app works offline. It contains no personal information and is removed when you uninstall the app. Google's handling of the transaction is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

You can view, manage or cancel a subscription at any time in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

6. Data we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in or personal profile.
  • No card content or card identifiers sent off the device.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.
  • No contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, photos or files.
  • No precise (GPS) location.

7. Data collected by third parties

The only third-party data collection in either app comes from the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the free app, and from Google Play Billing in the Pro app. The table below summarises it.

8. Your choices

  • Delete a single card from its details screen.
  • Delete all cards from Settings → Data.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Cancel a subscription in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Uninstall the app to remove every locally stored card and the stored purchase status.

Because the apps have no account and no server, there is no server-side data to request or delete. Uninstalling the app is the complete deletion.

9. Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect data from them.
