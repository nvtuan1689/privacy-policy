1. Overview

Dev4U Torch (the “App”) is a flashlight application. Its main function is to turn your device's camera flash (LED) into a bright flashlight, to use your screen as a light with adjustable brightness, and to provide Strobe, SOS and Morse code blinking patterns and a home-screen widget.

This policy explains what information the App accesses and why. In short:

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. The App has no account system, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no Internet access. Everything stays on your device.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, ID, …)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, calendar, messages, or call history
  • Photos, videos, audio, or files on your device
  • Device identifiers, usage statistics, or diagnostics

Because the App does not request Internet permission (Android) and makes no network connections (iOS), there is no mechanism for any data — personal or otherwise — to leave your device.

3. Camera & flashlight

The App uses the camera only as a source of light. When you switch on the flashlight, the App turns on the camera flash/LED. Specifically:

  • The camera sensor is never opened to take or record a picture or video.
  • No image, frame, or pixel data is ever read, stored, or transmitted.
  • Android: the App toggles the LED through the system CameraManager API and therefore requests no camera permission at all.
  • iOS: the App toggles the built-in flashlight (AVCaptureDevice). iOS may show the standard camera-access notice because the flash belongs to the camera hardware — this access is used only to power the flashlight.

In short: we access the camera flash to give you light, and nothing more.

4. Permissions we use

Below are the permissions the App can request, and why each one is used.

PlatformPermissionWhy we use it
Android
iOS		 Camera / Camera flash To power the flashlight (LED flash). Never used to capture or record anything.
Android Notifications
(POST_NOTIFICATIONS)		 To show a notification while the flashlight is on, so you can see its status and turn it off quickly.
Android Foreground service
(FOREGROUND_SERVICE)		 To keep the flashlight working and updating its notification when the App is in the background or the screen is off.
Android Wake lock
(WAKE_LOCK)		 To keep the screen awake while using strobe/SOS/Morse or the screen-light feature, so the light is not interrupted.
Android Home-screen widget To add a widget with a single on/off button. No data is shared with the widget.

Permissions are requested only when needed for the function you are using, and are never used for data collection. If you deny them, the related feature simply will not work (for example, notifications cannot be shown).

5. Data stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of settings locally on your device so your preferences are remembered:

  • Your selected language;
  • Your screen-light settings and preferred brightness;
  • Any Morse code presets you create or edit.

This information is kept in the App's own private storage on your device only. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared. Uninstalling the App (or clearing its data) removes it permanently.

6. Third-party services, ads & analytics

  • No advertising — there are no ads or ad SDKs.
  • No analytics — we do not use crash reporters or analytics SDKs.
  • No trackers — the App contains no tracking or fingerprinting code.
  • No third-party data — nothing is sent to any third party.

7. Security

Since the App transmits no data and holds only local settings, there is nothing to intercept over a network. Local preferences are protected by the operating system's standard app-sandboxing.

8. Children's privacy

The App is safe for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region).

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data, we will update this page and revise the “Effective date” at the top. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
